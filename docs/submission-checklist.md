# Submission asset checklist

This checklist prepares the version-controlled packet only. Every upload and
final form action remains manual.

## Final URLs and identity

- [ ] Replace the README and Devpost live-app placeholder with the final HTTPS URL.
- [ ] Confirm the public source URL resolves to the intended repository.
- [ ] Confirm the root MIT license is visible from the public repository.
- [ ] Upload the final English video manually and add its public YouTube URL.
- [ ] Record one full release SHA shared by deployment, README, video, and evidence.

## Draft and evidence

- [ ] Confirm the title and one-line summary in `devpost-submission.md`.
- [ ] Copy only fields that actually exist in the official Devpost form.
- [ ] Capture all five images in `screenshots.md` from the final release.
- [ ] Complete the asset-rights review and remove any unverified material.
- [ ] Verify the narrated video is under three minutes after upload.
- [ ] Classify payment proof as `live public` or `recorded local prototype`.
- [ ] Check that no evidence contains tokens, signatures, keys, seed phrases,
      environment dumps, full private wallet details, or unrelated profiles.

## Human-only actions

- [ ] Review the final Devpost preview manually.
- [ ] Confirm the public app, source, license, and video links from a signed-out browser.
- [ ] Submit only after the separate human release/submission checklist is complete.

No repository command publishes, uploads, or submits these materials.
